    About me

    I am a Chemical Engineer and Cosmetic Chemist with professional experience spanning research and development, quality control analysis, and product formulation in beauty and personal care manufacturing. I hold a Bachelor of Engineering degree in Chemical Engineering, with an academic foundation in chemical processes, transport phenomena, process design, reaction engineering and materials science. My multidisciplinary training and industry experience at Afri Botanica, Inveo Laboratories and PZ Cussons has equipped me with an understanding of both the scientific and practical dimensions of cosmetic product development.

    My research interests center on transdermal and topical delivery systems for bioactive compounds, hair fiber science and scalp biology, and hyperpigmentation in skin of colour; with my most recent work on the evidence gaps in chemical- versus UV-induced post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation in skin of colour. I have hands-on capability in the development of evidence-based products for melanin-rich skin and textured hair, ingredient compatibility assessment, stability evaluation, quality testing, and ethnobotanical research.

    For me, the next frontier in cosmetic innovation is not simply developing more products, but researching better science to make those products safer, more effective and more inclusive.

    Research

    Manuscript under review, Nigerian Journal of Dermatology

    A Scoping Review of the Evidence Gap in Chemical- versus UV-Induced Post-Inflammatory Hyperpigmentation Pathways in Skin of Colour

    Nwosu Precious Chinenyenwa (lead author) and Ekanem Mary Magdalene, 2026

    Clinical guidance often treats chemical and UV triggers of PIH as interchangeable. Following PRISMA-ScR, this review mapped the evidence behind that assumption across PubMed, Dimensions.ai and Cochrane CENTRAL, comparing cytokine, reactive oxygen species and tyrosinase pathway data in Fitzpatrick IV–VI skin.

    730unique records screened
    74studies included
    34%on acne-treatment chemical PIH
    12%on UV PIH by skin type

    The result is a strongly asymmetric, largely extrapolated evidence base. The review proposes a minimum reporting standard so future studies disaggregate by Fitzpatrick type.

    Emulsion and structuring systems

    Project 1 · Developed at Afri Botanica R&D

    Hydrating Body Lotion

    pH 5.5Oil-in-water

    Project overview

    An oil-in-water moisturising body lotion developed to provide skin hydration and softness through a combination of emollients, humectants and skin-conditioning ingredients at a pH of 5.5.

    Rationale

    • Emulsion system: LotionPro 165, cetyl alcohol and stearic acid for emulsification, viscosity development and structural support.
    • Emollients: Shea butter and sunflower oil for skin conditioning and emollience.
    • Hydration system: Glycerin, hyaluronic acid and panthenol for humectant support.
    • Rheology and preservation: Xanthan gum for viscosity modification, Disodium EDTA as chelator and Liquid Germall Plus as preservative.

    Project 2 · Developed at Afri Botanica R&D

    Emulsified Body Scrub

    Rinse-offMulti-particle exfoliant

    Project overview

    Development of an emulsified exfoliating body scrub combining sugar and particulate exfoliants with a rich conditioning system. The formulation integrates cleansing agents and emollients to provide physical exfoliation alongside a creamy, rinse-off application.

    Rationale

    • Exfoliating system: Combination of sugar, walnut shell and bamboo silica to create a multi-particle exfoliating system.
    • Emulsion and structuring: Emulsifying wax and stearic acid for emulsion formation and consistency.
    • Cleansing system: Alpha Olefin Sulfonate (AOS) powder and Cocamidopropyl betaine for cleansing and rinse-off properties.
    • Emollient system: Shea butter and sunflower oil for skin conditioning.
    • Humectant: Glycerin for moisture retention.

    Surfactant systems

    Project 3 · Developed at Afri Botanica R&D

    Clarifying Shampoo

    pH 5ASM 15%Pearlescent

    Project overview

    Development of a pearlescent, sulphate-based clarifying shampoo formulated with an anionic-amphoteric surfactant blend for cleansing performance, supported by conditioning polymers and a cooling agent for soothing washing experience. Formula has Active Surfactant Matter at 15% and pH of 5.

    Rationale

    • Surfactant system: Sodium Laureth Sulfate (SLES) and Cocamidopropyl Betaine (CAPB) for primary cleansing.
    • Conditioning system: Polyquaternium-7, Guar Hydroxypropyltrimonium Chloride, and Hydrolyzed Silk Protein for conditioning and nourished hair feel.
    • Pearlescent system: Ethylene Glycol Distearate heated in oil phase for opacity and pearlescence development.
    • Sensory modification: Menthol for a cooling sensation, with sweet orange and lavender essential oils for fragrance.
    • Viscosity and preservation: Sodium chloride for surfactant thickening and Liquid Germall Plus for preservation.

    Foaming Face Cleanser

    pH 5.5ASM 8%Mild

    Project overview

    A gentle foaming facial cleanser combining amphoteric and non-ionic surfactants to provide effective cleansing while maintaining a mild formulation profile and incorporating botanical ingredients. Formula has Active Surfactant Matter (ASM) at 8% and pH of 5.5.

    Rationale

    Project 5 · Developed at Afri Botanica R&D

    Anti-dandruff Shampoo

    pH 5ASM 20%Multi-active

    Project overview

    Development of a multi-active anti-dandruff shampoo combining an anionic-amphoteric-nonionic surfactant system with antifungal and keratolytic ingredients. The formulation was designed to support scalp cleansing and dandruff management while incorporating conditioning components. This formulation was set at an ASM of 20% and pH of 5.

    Rationale

    • Cleansing system: Sodium Lauroyl Sarcosinate, Alpha Olefin Sulfonate, Cocamidopropyl Betaine and Decyl Glucoside for complementary cleansing and foaming properties.
    • Anti-dandruff actives: Piroctone Olamine and Climbazole as antifungal agents, supported by salicylic acid as a keratolytic ingredient.
    • Solubilisation: Propylene Glycol incorporated to support the delivery of selected active ingredients.
    • Conditioning and structuring: Guar Hydroxypropyltrimonium Chloride, Cocamide MEA and PEG-150 Distearate for conditioning and viscosity development. Liquid pearliser for visual appeal.

    Cationic conditioning systems

    Project 6 · Developed at Afri Botanica R&D

    3-in-1 Hair Conditioner

    pH 4.5Detangle, deep and leave-in

    Project overview

    Development of a multifunctional conditioning formulation designed for detangling, deep conditioning and leave-in application at pH of 4.5. The formulation combines cationic conditioning agents, emollients, humectants and hydrolysed proteins to deliver maximum slip during detangling, protein replenishment for deep conditioning, both without build-up during rinse off, and moisture retention for leave-in application.

    Rationale

    • Conditioning system: BTMS-50, Polyquaternium-7 and cationic guar gum for conditioning, slip and improved detangling.
    • Emollient system: Shea butter, sunflower oil, jojoba oil and coco-caprylate for lubrication, softness and reduced friction.
    • Hydration system: Aloe vera juice, glycerin, sodium lactate and panthenol for humectant support.
    • Protein system: Hydrolysed silk and wheat proteins for hair conditioning and film-forming properties.
    • Emulsion structure: Stearic acid, Cetyl alcohol and Cetearyl alcohol/Ceteareth-20 for viscosity and structural development.

    Project 7 · Developed at Afri Botanica R&D

    Depigmenting Face Serum

    pH 5.0Aqueous serum

    Project overview

    Development of a multi-active facial serum formulated to address uneven skin tone and the appearance of hyperpigmentation at pH of 5.0. The aqueous formulation combines complementary brightening ingredients, humectants and skin-conditioning agents to support hydration and improve the appearance of uneven skin tone and hyperpigmentation.

    Rationale

    • Brightening actives: Niacinamide (4%), alpha-arbutin (3%), N-acetyl glucosamine (2%) and licorice root extract for complementary skin brightening mechanisms.
    • Hydration system: Glycerin, sodium hyaluronate, betaine and panthenol for humectant support and skin conditioning.
    • Skin soothing: Allantoin for soothing and conditioning properties.
    • Formulation support: Disodium EDTA as chelating agent and Liquid Germall Plus as preservative.

    Project 8 · Developed at Afri Botanica R&D

    Exfoliating Lip Treatment

    pH 4AHA + PHA

    Project overview

    Development of a water-based exfoliating lip treatment combining alpha-hydroxy acid and polyhydroxy acid technologies to support gentle surface exfoliation at a pH of 4. The formulation incorporates humectants, film-forming polymers and skin-conditioning ingredients to balance exfoliating functionality with moisture retention.

    Rationale

    • Exfoliating system: Lactic acid (2%) and gluconolactone (5%) for complementary exfoliating and humectant properties.
    • Humectant: Glycerin for moisture retention.
    • Rheology system: Hydroxyethylcellulose (HEC) and xanthan gum for viscosity development and gel structure.
    • Conditioning system: Allantoin and hydrolysed silk for skin conditioning.
    • Preservation: Euxyl PE 9010 for broad-spectrum preservation.

    Pre-poo Scalp Treatment

    pH 5Water-based

    Project overview

    Developed a water-based pre-poo scalp treatment combining exfoliating acids, sebum management ingredients and conditioning polymers at a pH of 5. The formulation is designed to support scalp cleansing preparation and improve the condition and manageability of hair before shampooing.
